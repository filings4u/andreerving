Affiliate Program Terms

Initial program rules · November 11, 2026

The Andre Erving Affiliate Program lets approved participants share eligible offerings and earn referral commissions. Applicants must provide accurate information and receive approval before links become active.

Approval and eligibility

Applying does not guarantee acceptance. We may decline or suspend accounts for fraud, misleading promotions, misuse of our brand, or violations of these rules. Affiliates must comply with applicable advertising, privacy, tax and consumer-protection rules.

Referral attribution

Each approved affiliate receives a unique referral link. We track a 30-day referral window by default. If multiple eligible affiliate links are used, the most recent valid tracked referral determines attribution. Browser settings, blocked storage, expired links and other technical limitations can prevent attribution. Invoices and purchases outside the supported tracked checkout may not be commission-eligible.

Commission calculations

The standard commission rate is 15% of an eligible verified sale, unless a different rate is assigned in your dashboard. Rates and promotional eligibility may change for future sales. Commissions are calculated on the successful payment amount and recorded as pending until reviewed. There is a standard 30-day hold to check for returns, fraud, chargebacks and other adjustments.

Disallowed activity

Self-referrals, artificial clicks, fake orders, unauthorized paid ads using our brand, unsolicited spam, and deceptive earnings or product claims are not allowed. Referrals resulting in refunds, payment disputes or fraudulent transactions are not payable.

Clear disclosures

Affiliates must clearly explain that they may earn a commission when promoting referral links, close to the link or recommendation. Example: “I may receive a commission if you purchase through my link.” Do not imply that Andre Erving guarantees income, a job, or a business outcome.

Payouts

The standard minimum payout is $50 in approved earnings. Stripe Connect payouts are planned but not yet activated. No affiliate payment is guaranteed until the correct payment processor account is configured, recipients complete applicable verification and tax requirements, and a transfer is confirmed. Payouts will be handled only after this setup is complete.

Program changes and support

Program settings and eligibility rules may be updated prospectively. Material changes will be communicated to affected partners. For questions or commission disputes, contact support@andreerving.com.
