How coaching works

Built around your decisions—not a generic script.

Andre's current coaching offer is positioned around personal and professional development, clear goal setting, practical strategies, and the willingness to challenge comfort zones.

01

Define the outcome

Get clear on what you're trying to change, build, decide, or accomplish.

02

Find the friction

Identify the habits, assumptions, gaps, or business constraints slowing progress.

03

Build the strategy

Turn the goal into a sequence of realistic actions with priorities and checkpoints.

04

Review and adjust

Use accountability and honest feedback to keep the plan useful as conditions change.
