Accessibility

A website designed to be usable by more people.

Andre Erving, LLC is committed to improving digital accessibility and providing a clear way to report barriers.

Our approach

We aim to use semantic structure, readable contrast, keyboard-friendly navigation, descriptive link text, responsive layouts, form labels, and alternative text where appropriate.

Continuous improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. New content, third-party tools, videos, downloads, and embedded services may introduce limitations that require additional work.

Need assistance?

If you encounter a barrier or need information in another format, contact support@andreerving.com and include the page or content you were trying to use.

Third-party content

Some experiences may be provided by third parties. We encourage those providers to support accessible experiences but cannot control every aspect of their platforms.
