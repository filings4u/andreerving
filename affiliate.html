How commissions work

Approved partners start at a standard 15% commission on eligible purchases. The rate can be adjusted by program administrators. A standard 30-day holding period supports refunds, fraud checks and payment verification. There is no guarantee of earnings. Stripe Connect payouts will be activated after payment-account setup and review.

Referral promotions must clearly disclose the partnership (for example, “I may earn a commission if you purchase through my link”). Self-referrals, fraudulent sales, chargebacks and refunded orders do not qualify.
