Freight Broker Training

What this program covers

Learn how brokerage actually moves.

The goal is to understand the entire transaction—not just finding a load. That means knowing how to win shipper business, source carriers, document the shipment, manage risk, communicate, and close the billing cycle.

Curriculum

Learn the system, not just the vocabulary.

Organized around the decisions, documents, workflows, and operating knowledge you will actually use.

01

Freight brokerage foundation

Understand the broker role, the freight market, stakeholders, and daily operating responsibilities.

02

Authority and compliance

Learn the federal setup framework, broker authority, BOC-3, UCR, bond/trust concepts, and compliance responsibilities.

03

Modes and market dynamics

Understand equipment types, transportation modes, spot and contract freight, and the factors that influence pricing.

04

Sales and marketing

Prospect shippers, develop a value proposition, quote freight, negotiate terms, and build a professional brand.

05

Carrier sourcing

Identify, vet, onboard, and maintain productive carrier relationships.

06

Documents and operations

Work through shipper and carrier packets, rate confirmations, bills of lading, PODs, invoices, and operating workflows.

07

Technology

Understand TMS, load boards, tracking, and the basic technology stack used in brokerage operations.

08

Risk management

Build practices around carrier vetting, claims, disputes, double-brokering risk, and compliance.

09

LTL

Understand freight classification, density, CWT concepts, and LTL-specific workflows.

10

Scaling

Track trends, refine processes, develop a team, and plan for controlled growth.

11

End-to-end workflow

Connect prospecting, quoting, carrier sourcing, setup, tracking, documentation, and billing into one process.

Questions

Before you enroll.

Course details can change as training is updated. Confirm current access terms during checkout.

Do I need trucks to become a freight broker?

No. A freight broker arranges transportation between shippers and authorized carriers rather than operating the carrier equipment.

Does the course include regulatory setup?

It explains the core setup and compliance framework. Always confirm current federal and state requirements before operating.

Is there a certificate?

Current course options include a certificate of completion after required coursework is completed.
