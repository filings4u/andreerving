About Andre

A career built by learning, adapting, and building again.

Technology opened one door. Transportation opened another. Entrepreneurship and education connected the pieces.

01

A practical path through multiple industries.

Andre's public biography describes a Chicago upbringing and an early foundation in computer information systems. His professional path later moved into transportation, where he worked as a truck driver, trainer, owner-operator, and eventually a freight brokerage entrepreneur.

That combination of technology, operations, logistics, and business became the foundation for training programs designed to help other people understand how systems work—and how to turn knowledge into action.

Education & service

Lifelong learning is part of the brand—not a slogan.

His current public biography notes bachelor's and master's degrees from DeVry University and doctoral study in Public Administration at Walden University. It also highlights Roseland Education and Resource Network, an initiative focused on resources, mentoring, and support for entrepreneurs and underserved communities.

“Failure is your guide to success.”A resilience-centered idea reflected in Andre's public writing.
