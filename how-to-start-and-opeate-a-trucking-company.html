Trucking Pro

Build a working understanding of how to start, operate, and strengthen a trucking company—from formation and compliance to equipment, drivers, freight, finance, and growth.

What this program covers

From first setup to daily operations.

Trucking is not one decision—it is a system. This program organizes the moving parts so owners can understand how formation, equipment, safety, freight, people, documentation, and money connect.

Curriculum

Learn the system, not just the vocabulary.

Organized around the decisions, documents, workflows, and operating knowledge you will actually use.

01

Industry foundation

Understand the trucking market, its history, current structure, and business models.

02

Business formation

Choose a structure, register the company, and understand federal and state setup steps.

03

Business planning

Build a plan around market position, startup capital, operating costs, and financial projections.

04

Trucks and trailers

Compare equipment types, new versus used equipment, and financing considerations.

05

Marketing and shippers

Build a marketing plan, identify target customers, develop shipper relationships, and bid on opportunities.

06

Rates and pricing

Separate fixed and operating costs and build rate-per-mile and contract pricing strategies.

07

New Entrant readiness

Understand the New Entrant process, audit expectations, and core documents.

08

Drivers and qualification

Recruit and onboard drivers while understanding screening, PSP, physicals, and qualification files.

09

Drug & alcohol compliance

Understand Clearinghouse, random testing, consortium responsibilities, and MIS reporting.

10

Licensing, insurance, IRP & IFTA

Work through licensing, insurance, apportioned registration, and fuel-tax responsibilities.

11

Maintenance & inspections

Build preventative maintenance, DVIR, inspection, ELD, HOS, and CSA awareness into operations.

12

Company operations

Design departments, staffing, load sourcing, broker relationships, accounting, documentation, and billing.

13

Technology & location

Use GPS, dash cameras, tracking, mobile applications, and plan an appropriate operating location.

14

Growth & investment

Prepare for scaling, investor conversations, stronger reporting, and disciplined decision-making.

Questions

Before you enroll.

Course details can change as training is updated. Confirm current access terms during checkout.

Is this only for people who have not started yet?

No. The course is designed for people preparing to launch as well as owners who want a more organized operating framework.

Is the course live?

The core course is self-paced. Coaching or cohort options, when offered, may have scheduled sessions.

Does completing the course replace legal or regulatory advice?

No. Regulations and filing requirements can change. Use the training as education and verify current requirements with the appropriate agencies and professionals.
